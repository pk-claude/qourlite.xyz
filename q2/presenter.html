If something goes sideways

Voting will not connect (all dots red). Corporate networks sometimes block the free relays. Try your phone's hotspot for the laptop. If that is not an option, run it as a show of hands: read the question, take answers in the Teams chat, press → to reveal. Every animation still works.
Someone cannot join. Have them open qourlite.xyz/join on their phone using mobile data (not office Wi-Fi), or on their laptop browser.
Something inappropriate shows up on the word wall. Click it. It disappears.
You refreshed by accident. Nothing is lost. The quiz comes back where you were.
Rehearse tonight. Menu → Rehearse with bots adds 12 pretend players who answer on their own. Then Menu → Reset before the real thing.

What is on this site, and what is not

Only public information is on qourlite.xyz: the Q2 release, the 10-Q, the earnings call, Lowe's public results, and public macro data. Internal numbers from your deck (MIP payouts, plan comparisons, division and department comps, the RDC reject rate, internal macro-pack figures) were left off on purpose. They stay in your PowerPoint. The pages are marked noindex so search engines skip them.
