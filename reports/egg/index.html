Known limitations

The dated facts are not in dispute at the precision this question needs; the scoring rules are. Each line of evidence is mapped to a bounded advantage by a hand-written rule (Appendix rows tagged ASM), and the composite weights are preferences. The speciation input is a caricature: real species boundaries are population-level and fuzzy, and the "single mutation" options exist to make the possessive reading well-posed, not because anyone thinks a chicken appeared in one generation. Domestication dates carry a 3,500 - 9,500 year spread between the zooarchaeological and genomic estimates; the report exposes that as a slider rather than choosing. The amniote-origins line rests on one 2023 phylogenetic study whose authors flag taphonomic bias. Nothing here was measured for this report.
