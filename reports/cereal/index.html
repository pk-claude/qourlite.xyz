Known limitations

Flakes are treated as a three-layer raft (submerged / capillary-bridged / dry) rather than as individuals. The raft does not thin as it is eaten. Spoon agitation wets neighbors; the model charges every bite the same 3 s dip and ignores collateral wetting. Within-flake moisture transport is lumped into one first-order rate. Pouring is treated as one continuous stream at a constant rate. Sensory crispness is a Fermi function of moisture, which reproduces the sharp loss reported in cereal texture studies but is not calibrated to any specific product. Composite weights are preferences, not physics.
