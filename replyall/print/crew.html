Operator + Host

page 2 of 3
Operatorat the laptop
facing the host
One rule: press the button with the yellow ring + NOW tag.
  1. Setup. Open big screen, test sound, show join code, approve 1-2 buzzer phones per team.
  2. Rules. Host reads each slide. Press Next slide when they stop talking.
  3. Each round. Show question, Open buzzers, then enter each answer the team gives. Wrong answer = Strike.
  4. Call-ups after Round 2. Promote the top audience player, then run the pitch-off vote.
  5. You said. Open survey, Close and count, tidy answers, put it on the board.
  6. Final + winner. Type what players say, Space to reveal. Then Reveal the MVP.
Keys
1-8 reveal   X strike   Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo   Phones fail: left half of keyboard buzzes team 1, right half team 2.
Hostbeside the screen
facing the room
You talk, read and rule. Never wait in silence.
  1. Welcome. "Phones out. Scan the code. Join as Just the audience."
  2. Each board. Read the question. "Audience, call your guess now." Call up the face-off.
  3. Rule out loud. Use the four signals. You decide what counts.
  4. Close. Thank the teams, promoted players and bingo winners.
Rules script, one line per slide
1 How to play"Guess the popular answer, not the right one."
2 Face-off"One per team. Fastest buzz answers first. Better answer wins the board."
3 Three strikes"Answer one at a time. Three misses and the other team gets one guess to steal it all."
4 Audience"You play too. Guess before the first reveal. On the board, the points are yours."
5 Get called up"After Round 2, two of you join the teams: the top phone scorer, then the pitch-off winner, +50."
6 The stakes"Round 3 pays double. You write the next board for triple. Then the final round."

Wrangler + Tech + Test day

page 3 of 3
Wranglerbetween the teams
  1. Before. Two teams of 3-5. Pick 1-2 buzzer phones per team. Set the face-off order for Rounds 1-3.
  2. Each round. Send the next pair up as the question is read. Phones fail? Shout who buzzed first.
  3. Call-ups. Screen says PROMOTED: walk that person to their team. Have 2 pitch-off volunteers ready early; time 30 sec each.
  4. Final. Take player two out of earshot; bring them back on cue. Line both teams up for the winner.
Techback row, then roaming
  1. Plug in. Power, projector, speakers. Display = Extend. PC Win+P. Mac: Displays, mirroring off.
  2. Wi-Fi. Test on laptop and a phone. Post the Wi-Fi name by the flyers.
  3. Sound. Stand in the back row during Test voice. Signal louder or softer.
  4. Join help. Same Wi-Fi, reload, Meeting ID matches the screen. Frozen screen: click it, F5 (Mac Cmd+R).

Test day checklist

  • Host picked, has this handout
  • Room with screen + HDMI
  • Run Watch a demo show once
  • Laptop on power, display Extend
  • Test sound + voice
  • Everyone joins, buzzers approved
  • Practice the 4 signals
  • Time each round
  • Note every "what now?" moment
  • Note phone failures (iPhone / Android)
  • Try the pitch-off
  • Ask: confusing? funny? dragged?

Pass = one full game, start to winner, no reset.
