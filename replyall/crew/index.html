Role cards

Operator

at the laptop
facing the host
One rule: press the button with the yellow ring + NOW tag. The bar on top says what it does.
  1. SetupOpen big screen, test sound, show join code, approve 1-2 buzzer phones per team, name the teams.
  2. RulesHost reads each slide. Press Next slide when they stop.
  3. Each roundShow question, Open buzzers, click the face-off winner, then answers or Strike.
  4. Call-upsAfter Round 2: promote top audience player, run the pitch-off vote.
  5. You saidOpen survey, Close and count, tidy answers, put it on the board.
  6. Final + winnerType what players say, Space to reveal. Then Reveal the MVP.

Shortcuts

  1. 1-8 reveal answerX strike / Ctrl+Z or Cmd+Z undo
  2. Phones fail?Buzzers open: left half of keyboard = Team 1, right half = Team 2.

Host

beside the screen
facing the room
You talk, read and rule. You never touch the laptop. Never wait in silence.
  1. Welcome"Phones out. Scan the code. Join as Just the audience."
  2. RulesRead the 6 slides (script below). Stop talking = next slide.
  3. Each boardRead the question. "Audience, call your guess now." Call up the face-off.
  4. Rule on answersUse the 4 signals. You decide what counts.
  5. Call-ups + finalRead the call-up and final round lines in the host script.
  6. CloseThank the teams, promoted players, bingo winners.
Rules script: 6 slides, read aloud
1 How to play

"You are not guessing the right answer. You are guessing the popular one."

2 Face-off

"One player per team. Fastest buzz answers first. Better answer wins the board."

3 Three strikes

"Answer one at a time. Three misses and the other team gets one guess to steal it all."

4 Audience

"You play too. Call a guess before the first reveal. On the board, the points are yours."

5 Get called up

"After Round 2, two of you join the teams. The top phone scorer is promoted. Then a pitch-off: 30 seconds each, you vote, the winner joins with 50 points."

6 The stakes

"Round 3 pays double. You write the next board for triple. Then the final round."

Wrangler

between the teams
near the front
You move people so the host never has to stop and organize.

Before

  1. Split two teams3-5 each. Names to Pavel.
  2. Buzzer phones1-2 per team join as their team. Tell Pavel whose to approve.
  3. Face-off orderWho steps up in Round 1, 2, 3. No arguing on stage.

During

  1. Send the next pairAs the host reads the question.
  2. Phones fail?Shout who buzzed first. Pavel enters it.
  3. PromotionScreen says PROMOTED: walk that person to their team.
  4. Pitch-offHave 2 volunteers ready early. Time 30 sec each.
  5. Final roundTake player two out of earshot. Bring back on cue.
  6. WinnerLine both teams up by the screen.

Tech

back of the room
then roaming
The room must see, hear and join. Everything else is someone else's job.

30 minutes before

  1. Plug inPower, projector, speakers. Display = Extend, not Duplicate. PC: Win+P. Mac: Displays, mirroring off.
  2. Wi-FiTest on laptop and a phone. Post the Wi-Fi name and password by the QR.
  3. Big screenDrag it to the projector, click once for full screen.
  4. Sound checkStand in the back row during Test voice. Signal louder / softer.

During

  1. Join helpSame Wi-Fi, reload, Meeting ID matches the screen.
  2. Frozen screenClick it, then F5 (Mac Cmd+R). It reconnects.
